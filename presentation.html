The Industry Problem

Fragile Financial Ledgers Under Concurrency

Why traditional mobile wallets fail under real-world network packet loss and concurrent load
02 / 10
17.4%
Floating-Point Drift

Standard software uses floating-point decimals (`0.1 + 0.2 = 0.30000000000000004`). In high-frequency ledger bursts, tiny rounding errors compound into critical balance discrepancies and phantom money.

2x Double
Charges on Packet Loss

Mobile networks drop packets during payment submission. Without strict idempotent write paths and distributed key hashing, user retries trigger double withdrawals and balance drain.

Destructive
Retroactive Overwrites

Traditional databases mutate rows in place when corrections occur (`UPDATE balance`), destroying the audit trail and making it impossible to reconstruct historical balances as of a past timestamp.

The Pocketpay Solution

Mathematical Conservation Meets Bitemporality

Strict double-entry invariants backed by dual-timeline SQLite WAL ledger
03 / 10
$\sum \Delta = 0$
Zero-Sum Ledger Conservation

Every transfer strictly debits the sender and credits the receiver in atomic transactions. Enforced at the SQL engine level with `CHECK (balance >= 0)` to mathematically prevent negative balances.

Dual-Time
Bitemporal Revision Engine

Separates Effective Time (`effective_at` / when funds actually moved) from Recorded Time (`recorded_at` / when the system learned of it), enabling audit-proof time-travel queries.

Zero Drift
Integer Minor Units & Idempotency

All currency amounts stored as integer minor units (cents). 5-path distributed idempotency engine tracks canonical request hashes to guarantee safe retries under unreliable networks.

Technical Moat

Dual-Timeline Bitemporal Accounting

Reconstructing the past with perfect fidelity while preventing retroactive overdrafts
07 / 10
Effective Time vs Recorded Time

• as_of (Effective Time): When the financial event took place in the real world.
• known_at (Recorded Time): When the system recorded or amended that transaction.
• Half-Open Intervals [from, to): Statements cover exact windows without boundary double-counting.
• Snapshot Pagination: Opaque tokens anchor client pagination to a frozen view of history, preventing phantom items during active scrolling.

Historical Overdraft Protection Invariant

When an authorized operator retroactively amends or reverses a transaction from the past, the ledger evaluates the reconstructed balance at every historical boundary point between then and now.

If the account balance would have dropped below zero ($B(t) < 0$) at even a single millisecond in the past, the revision is rejected with HTTP 409 `historical_overdraft`.
